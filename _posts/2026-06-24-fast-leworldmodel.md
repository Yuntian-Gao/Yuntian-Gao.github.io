Our paper **Fast LeWorldModel** is now available on arXiv.

Fast-LeWM replaces repeated autoregressive latent rollout with action-prefix prediction. Given the current latent and a candidate action sequence, it predicts future latents for multiple action prefixes in parallel, reducing planning time and accumulated long-horizon error.

**Authors:** Yuntian Gao, Xiangyu Xu

## Fast LeWorldModel

A fast latent world model for visual planning. Fast-LeWM uses action-prefix prediction to evaluate future latents in parallel, reducing planning time while improving average success over LeWorldModel.

[[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26217)] [[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26217)]

## RoboMaster Vision Systems

Vision algorithm development for RoboMaster competitions. This work contributed to National First Prizes in the 2023 and 2024 RoboMaster University Championship Super Confrontation.

## 2026

**Fast LeWorldModel**<br>
**Yuntian Gao**, Xiangyu Xu<br>
arXiv, 2026. [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26217)] [[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26217)]

Fast-LeWM replaces repeated local latent rollout with action-prefix prediction. It predicts future latents for multiple action prefixes in parallel, improving planning efficiency and reducing long-horizon latent error.

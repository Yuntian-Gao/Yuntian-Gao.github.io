## About Me

I am **Yuntian Gao (高云天)**, a first-year M.Eng. student at [Xi'an Jiaotong University](https://en.xjtu.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. Xiangyu Xu. My research focuses on **3D Computer Vision** and **World Models**.

## Research Interests
